A whole computer running in a pixel shader on your own graphics card: a 256-core RISC-V machine that boots real Linux to a desktop. Play Doom, Quake, Quake III, Half-Life: Uplink, Jedi Outcast, Red Alert, Command & Conquer, Age of Empires, OpenTTD, chess and a NES; browse the web, play music and video; write, build and debug C in its IDE. Sit in a holodeck and a game's 3D world is round you. Every visitor runs a machine of their own, and can show its screen to the others on a classroom computer. Note: this is usually VERY slow depending on your GPU. Open source: github.com/MichaelMoroz/ShaderEmu
