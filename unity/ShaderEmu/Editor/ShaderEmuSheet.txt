head: THE MACHINE
Processor | 32-bit RISC-V (RV32IMAF), 256 cores: the first runs Linux, 255 workers take shares of a program's work; a few million instructions a second, as fast as your graphics card runs it
Memory | 256 MB, kept in textures, as is its disk
System | Linux 5.17, BusyBox, musl; Nano-X windows; its own GPU (OpenGL ES) and sound card; a network between the players' machines; /persistence kept between visits (SAVE on the sign by the power button)

head: HOW TO START
POWER on the tower right of the desk (or the control panel); then the desktop's icons, the Start menu (Win key), or the console's shell. The holodecks through the left wall's doorway: sit, and a 3D game is around you. Every player runs a machine of their own; Reset starts it over.
