ShaderEmu Web

A browser for simple pages: text, links, lists, pictures, and the plainer part of style sheets (colours, backgrounds, spacing, what is hidden). No scripts, and nothing side by side: a page made of columns comes out as one.

In VRChat the addresses listed on this page open at a click; any other needs one step by hand. VRChat does not let a world open an address by itself, only those it was made with and one a visitor gives it. So when you follow a link inside a site, or type an address above, a button appears by the display: it opens a panel with the address to copy and a field to paste it into, and the page opens. Its style sheets and pictures ask the same way, up to three at a time. That is a rule of VRChat, not a fault of this computer.
